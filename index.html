Smart Shop Bill

Business Problem: Take product name, price and quantity. Calculate total bill.

Formula / Logic: Total = Price × Quantity
Allowed: input(), print(), variables, int(), float() and arithmetic operators. No if-else, loops or functions.

Assignment 19

Loan EMI Practice

Business Problem: Take principal, annual interest rate and tenure in years. Calculate an approximate simple monthly payment using simple interest for beginner practice.

Formula / Logic: Interest = P × R × T / 100; Monthly = (P + Interest) / (12 × T)
Allowed: input(), print(), variables, int(), float() and arithmetic operators. No if-else, loops or functions.
p = float(input("Enter loan amount: "))
r = float(input("Enter annual interest rate: "))
t = float(input("Enter tenure in years: "))
interest = p * r * t / 100
total = p + interest
monthly = total / (12 * t)
print("Total Repayment: ₹", total)
print("Approx. Monthly Payment: ₹", monthly)
